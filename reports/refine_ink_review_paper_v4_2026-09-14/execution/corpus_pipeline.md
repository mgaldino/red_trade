## Item 8 — Validation does not establish trade-category accuracy

### Original comment

> Table 22’s 88 percent agreement appears to be an unweighted result from a category-stratified sample and therefore may not estimate corpus-wide accuracy. Moreover, the six concordant `china-brazil trade` cases identify only one side of class performance unless the stratification basis and full confusion matrix are reported; they do not establish both precision and recall or rule out time-varying misclassification in the category trend underlying Figure 6.

### Current locations

- `paper_v4.Rmd:2074-2119`: validation description, Table 22 construction, and interpretation.
- `scripts/functions.R:5623-5646`: Cohen's kappa and observed agreement are computed without sampling weights.
- `scripts/functions.R:5648-5688`: Table 22 groups by `chatgpt_label` and calculates `mean(match)`; the overall row is the unweighted `mean(match)` over all 100 rows.
- `_targets.R:756-759`: the annotated CSV is a file target, followed by summary and kappa targets. There is no target for the sample draw, design metadata, confusion matrix, inclusion probabilities, class precision/recall, or temporal error.
- `data/folha_validation_sample_annotated.csv`: 100 annotated rows, 88 exact label matches, prediction-stratum sizes ranging from 5 to 25, and coverage from 2001 to 2014. No sampling identifiers, weights, seed, or inclusion probabilities are stored.

### Status: CONFIRMED

Defect evidence:

- The 88 percent figure is mechanically an unweighted validation-sample agreement rate. Because the archived materials do not establish the allocation and within-stratum draw mechanism, its relationship to corpus-wide accuracy is unknown.
- The category rows condition on `chatgpt_label`. They are empirical agreement rates within predicted-label strata; calling them generic category accuracy obscures the conditioning direction.
- The trade cell has 6 true positives, 0 false positives, and 1 false negative in the archived validation file. Table 22 exposes only the 6 predictions labeled trade and therefore omits the false negative needed for recall. Six predicted positives and seven manually positive cases are too few for a strong population-performance claim.
- The current sample contains 55 observations from 2001-2008 and 45 from 2009-2014, with 12 total disagreements, but it was not stratified by time, omits 2000, and has no category-by-period design. These cells cannot rule out time-varying error in the annual series behind Figure 6.
- A bounded search of current R scripts, R Markdown files, and the target graph found no producer for the validation draw and no preserved random seed or inclusion-probability calculation.

Refuting evidence: none for the central defect. The full annotated CSV does allow a diagnostic confusion matrix to be reconstructed, but that matrix is not reported and does not recover the missing sample design.

Reasoning: the table correctly reports the arithmetic agreement observed in the 100 archived rows. Its title and prose overinterpret that arithmetic as accuracy without identifying the target population or sampling weights. The trade row describes only the predicted-trade side of performance, and the sample design cannot sustain a temporal-stability claim.

Proposed-fix assessment: **needs_design** for new weighted, class-specific, or temporal metrics. The interpretation-only replacements below are safe immediately; numerical additions should enter through `targets` after authorization.
