Moratorium on the use of the death penalty
Before operative paragraph 1, insert a new operative paragraph reading:
1. Affirms the sovereign right of States to determine the legal measures and
penalties which are appropriate in their societies, including the death penalty
for the most serious crimes, in accordance with international law;
and renumber the subsequent paragraphs accordingly.
