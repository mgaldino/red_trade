Agenda item 96
General and complete disarmament
Belarus, Brazil, Kazakhstan, Nepal, Qatar, Tajikistan and Turkmenistan:
draft resolution
International Day for a World Free of Nuclear Weapons
The General Assembly,
Recalling that the promotion of peace and security is among the main purposes
and principles of the United Nations embodied in its Charter,
Convinced that every effort should be made to prevent nuclear war and
catastrophes in order to avert devastating effects on humankind, the environment
and our planet,
Recalling the past accomplishments and new initiatives towards the
establishment of a world free of nuclear weapons, including specific efforts by
Member States and civil society,
Welcoming the recent positive momentum in the international community to
work towards this goal,
Acknowledging the potential of education as a tool for peace, security,
disarmament and non-proliferation,
Aware of the important role of youth in society in contributing to the
promotion of the common ends of peace and security,
Emphasizing the essential role of Governments, intergovernmental
organizations, civil society, academia and mass media in promoting these ideals to
youth,
1. Declares 29 August as the International Day for a World Free of Nuclear
Weapons, devoted to enhancing public awareness and education about nuclear
disarmament and non-proliferation;
2. Invites Member States, main bodies, relevant agencies, funds and
programmes of the United Nations, regional commissions, intergovernmental, inter-
parliamentary and non-governmental organizations and individuals to commemorateA/C.1/64/L.14

in an appropriate manner the International Day for a World Free of Nuclear
Weapons, especially through all means of educational and public awareness-raising
activities, and to cooperate with the United Nations in the observance of the
International Day;
3. Proposes the use of thematic conferences, round tables, seminars,
documentaries, publications, exhibits, educational and training programmes and
other activities designed for and/or by youth, as well as innovative social
networking and media technologies on an ongoing basis so that youth are better
informed for engagement in a constructive dialogue and action with Member States,
governmental organizations, civil society, academia and mass media;
4. Requests the Secretary-General to invite Member States, the Secretariat,
organizations of the United Nations system, relevant non-governmental
organizations and educational institutions to support the observance of the
International Day as a global spotlight in the context of a range of year-round
continuing awareness-raising and educational programmes on nuclear disarmament
and non-proliferation issues and events highlighting significant milestones and
achievements towards a world free of nuclear weapons;
5. Also requests the Secretary-General to submit to the General Assembly at
its sixty-sixth session a report on the implementation of the present resolution.
