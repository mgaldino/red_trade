I. Requests contained in the draft resolution
1. Under the terms of the operative paragraph of draft resolution
A/C.3/63/L.57/Rev.1, the General Assembly would take note of the report of the
Human Rights Council (A/63/53 and Add.1) and endorse the recommendations
contained therein.
II. Relationship of the draft resolution to the programme of work for
the biennium 2008-2009
2. The activities to be carried out under the terms of the resolution relate to:
(a) programme 1, General Assembly and Economic and Social Council affairs and
conference management; (b) subprogramme 2, Supporting human rights bodies and
organs, of programme 19, Human rights; and (c) subprogramme 4, Support services,
of programme 24, Management and support services, of the biennial programme
plan and priorities for the period 2008-2009. They also fall under section 2, General
Assembly and Economic and Social Council affairs and conference management,
section 23, Human rights, and section 28E, Administration, Geneva, of the
programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009.A/C.3/63/L.77

III. Activities through which the requests would be implemented and
additional requirements
3. The programme budget implications set out in the present report relate solely
to the recommendations contained in the resolutions and decisions adopted by the
Human Rights Council at its ninth session, as contained in its report to the General
Assembly (A/63/53/Add.1).
4. At its ninth session, the Human Rights Council adopted a number of
resolutions and decisions by which it authorized relevant human rights bodies and
special procedures to continue their activities, or undertake additional activities,
including meetings, in excess of the resources approved in the programme budget
for the biennium 2008-2009. In accordance with rule 153 of the rules of procedure
of the General Assembly, the Council was provided, where appropriate, with a
statement of programme budget implications prior to the adoption of those
resolutions and decisions.
5. A summary of the requirements arising from the resolutions and decisions
adopted by the Human Rights Council at its ninth session is contained in the annex
to the present report. It should be noted that Council resolutions 9/1, 9/3, 9/14, 9/15,
9/17 and 9/19 and the presidential statement of 24 September 2008 (PRST 9/1),
would require a total amount of $1,681,700, for the implementation of activities that
are considered to be of a “perennial nature”. This amount has been provided for in
the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009. With regard to Council
resolutions 9/2, 9/3, 9/7, 9/18 and its decision 9/101, no provisions have been made
in the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009 for the additional
requirements of $467,600. These requirements will be absorbed, to the extent
possible, and will be addressed in the context of the second performance for the
biennium 2008-2009. The requirements for the biennium 2010-2011 will be
addressed in the context of the proposed programme budget for the biennium 2010-
2011.
6. In its decision 9/103, the Human Rights Council requested the Secretary-
General to present to the General Assembly during the main part of its sixty-third
session, a report with details of the resources required to ensure the provision of
necessary services indicated in the report of the Office of the United Nations High
Commissioner for Human Rights (OHCHR) on conference facilities and financial
support for the Council (A/HRC/9/18) and recommended that the General Assembly
ensure the establishment of an office of the President of the Human Rights Council,
with adequate staffing resources.
7. With regard to decision 9/103, in its above-mentioned report, the Office of the
High Commissioner informed the Human Rights Council that additional staff
resources, provisionally estimated at approximately $7,500,000 per year, would be
required to ensure the appropriate level of conference support required by the
Council, including requirements for additional infrastructure and webcasting.
Following further review and analysis of the additional resource requirements, the
amount has been revised to $6,130,200 (gross), to be offset by an amount of
$231,900 under staff assessment or $5,898,300 (net) under the programme budget
for the biennium 2008-2009.A/C.3/63/L.77

A. Section 2. General Assembly and Economic and Social Council affairs and
conference management
9. Under section 2 of the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009,
General Assembly and Economic and Social Council affairs and conference
management, the increase in the level of documentation arising from the
requirements of the Human Rights Council has resulted in an increase of 26 per cent
in the translation workload of the Conference Services Division of the United
Nations Office at Geneva, as compared to the previous biennium, and is also the
main cause of the expected increase in the workload in editorial services and in the
production of summary records.
10. In order to process the additional 5,370 pages of documentation estimated in
2009 for the three sessions on the universal periodic review and to produce
summary records for the additional 142 meetings, the language services of the
Division would require: (a) two additional P-5 posts for Senior Revisers to deal withA/C.3/63/L.77

increased volume of documentation to be translated under contractual arrangements;
(b) two P-4 Revisers and six P-3 Translators to augment the capacity of the English
and French Translation Sections to cover the production of summary records for the
additional 10 weeks of the sessions of the Human Rights Council (100 fully serviced
meetings), the Committee on the Rights of Persons with Disabilities (20 meetings
with summary records) and the Committee on Enforced Disappearances
(22 meetings with summary records), as well as to deal with the summary records
backlog (in 2009, this estimated workload would represent a total of 142 additional
meetings requiring summary records); (c) three Editors (2 P-4 and 1 P-3), required
to augment the capacity of the Division to meet the increased editorial workload;
and (d) temporary assistance for meetings for documentation processing. In regard
to the latter requirement for temporary assistance, an annual additional provision of
$3,816,000 is required to handle work for which submission dates are not
predictable, as well as to accommodate activities of the Council, which, given its
entitlements, can be announced on short notice and may coincide with periods of
heavy workload. The requirement also takes into consideration the resources needed
to process the reports under the universal periodic review mechanism, which are
submitted to the Council as official documents.

$4,944,200 for the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009. The
requirements for the biennium 2010-2011, estimated at $11,851,500, will be
considered within the context of the proposed programme budget for the biennium
2010-2011.
B. Section 23. Human rights
12. Since the establishment of the Human Rights Council, the President of the
Council has been supported by the staff of his/her own mission. In addition, in
liaison and coordination with the Bureau of the Council, ad hoc support from
diplomats from other missions on loan arrangements has been provided to the
President when support was not available from his/her own mission.
13. As an intergovernmental body, the Presidency of the Human Rights Council is
subject to election on the basis of regional rotation. The Council secretariat at
OHCHR provides comprehensive technical and substantive support to the President,
his/her Bureau and to the Council as a whole. Such support does not, however,
extend to personal assistance functions relating to the scheduling and handling of
bilateral political meetings since the secretariat has the obligation to serve all
Members of the United Nations in a fair, objective and transparent manner. The
secretariat cannot be involved in political negotiations and cannot handle requests
that are outside its functions and responsibilities. Should one of their representatives
be elected to the Presidency of the Council in future, it is anticipated that some
small missions could face difficulties in providing sufficient support owing to lack
of resources and/or capacity.
14. In this connection, it is proposed that an independent office of the President of
the Human Rights Council would require two P-3 and one General Service (Other
level) staffing posts to manage the bilateral meetings of the President. These
additional resources, amounting to approximately $392,700 under general temporary
assistance, would be required under section 23, Human rights, of the programme
budget for the biennium 2008-2009. The resource requirements for the bienniumA/C.3/63/L.77

C. Section 27. Public information
15. It will be recalled that, since the establishment of the Human Rights Council,
various reports have been submitted to the General Assembly on ways and means to
provide the Council with the required services, including the provision of
webcasting and public information services.
16. With regard to the requirements for webcasting and public information
services, it will be recalled that, in his report on revised estimates relating to the
programme budget for the biennium 2006-2007 and to the proposed programme
budget for the biennium 2008-2009 under sections 2, 23, 27, 28E and 35 and income
section 1 and proposal related to unforeseen and extraordinary expenses arising
from the implementation of decisions of the Human Rights Council (A/62/125), the
Secretary-General outlined the programmatic and financial consequences of Human
Rights Council decision 3/104. In that context, the Secretary-General indicated that
resources amounting to $1,356,800 would be required under section 27, Public
information, in the biennium of 2008-2009, for the provision of press, radio,
television, photo coverage and webcast transmission, on a regular basis, of all
regular and special sessions of the Human Rights Council. By its resolution 62/238,
the General Assembly endorsed the conclusions and recommendation of the
Advisory Committee on Administrative and Budgetary Questions (see
A/62/7/Add.25), whereby the Committee noted the additional activities proposed
under section 27, as described in the report of the Secretary-General, and the
pragmatic approach being taken to the requirement for webcasting for the Human
Rights Council. However, taking into account the overall expenditure pattern under
section 27 for the current biennium, the Committee was of the opinion that no net
additional resource requirements should arise under section 27 for the biennium
2008-2009. Consequently, no additional appropriation was approved under section
27, Public information, of the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009.
17. It will also be recalled that at the time of adoption of draft resolution
A/C.3/62/L.84, entitled “Report of the Human Rights Council”, the Secretary-
General issued a statement of programme budget implications (A/C.5/62/12)
informing the General Assembly that should the draft resolution be adopted,
additional resources amounting to $353,700 would be required under section 27,
Public information, in the biennium 2008-2009 for the provision of press, radio,
television, photo coverage and webcast transmission required arising from the
implementation of the Human Rights Council resolution 5/1, entitled “Institution-
building of the United Nations Human Rights Council”. By its resolution 62/238,
the General Assembly did not approve the additional resources under section 27,
Public information, of the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009.
18. Against this backdrop, in order to implement the provisions contained in draft
resolution of A/C.3/63/L.57/Rev.1, it is estimated that additional requirements
amounting to $221,900 would be required under section 27, Public information, of
the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009, which includes the proposed
establishment of one P-3 post for a Radio and Television Producer. The related
requirements would provide for information services, in particular those relating to
press, radio, television and photo coverage. For the biennium 2010-2011, theA/C.3/63/L.77

$579,000, will be considered within the context of the proposed programme budget

D. Section 28E. Administration, Geneva
20. As detailed above, the implementation of Human Rights Council decision
9/103 would give rise to the establishment of 14 posts, 13 posts under section 2,
General Assembly and Economic and Social Council affairs and conference
management, and one post under section 27, Public information. To provide
accommodation and common support service to the 14 proposed new posts,
estimated additional resources amounting to $339,500 would be required in 2009
under section 28E, Administration, Geneva, comprising:
(a) A one-time requirement of $225,600 for accommodation, furnishing and
office automation equipment and software;
(b) Recurrent requirements of $113,900 per year for rental of office space
($77,500), general operating expenses ($29,400) and office supplies and materials
($7,000).
21. These additional requirements of $339,500 would be required under the
programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009. In addition, the recurrent
requirements, estimated at $113,900, will be considered within the context of the
proposed programme budget for the biennium 2010-2011.

VI. Potential for absorption and the contingency fund
25. No provisions have been made under sections 2, 23, 27 and 28E of the
programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009 for the requirements resulting from
Human Rights Council decision 9/103.
26. It will be recalled that, under the procedures established by the General
Assembly in its resolutions 41/213 and 42/211, a contingency fund is established for
each biennium to accommodate additional expenditures derived from legislative
mandates not provided for in the programme budget. Under this procedure, if
additional expenditures were proposed that exceeded the resources available from
the contingency fund, the activities concerned would be implemented only through
the redeployment of resources from low-priority areas or the modification of
existing activities. Otherwise, such additional activities would have to be deferred to
a later biennium.
27. The Secretariat has sought to identify areas from which resources can be
redeployed to meet the additional requirements relating to the substantive support to
the Council, the conference-servicing and other support services requirements
referred to above. It is considered that additional requirements could be absorbed
within the provisions of section 23, Human rights ($467,600), of the programme
budget for the biennium 2008-2009. The balance of $5,898,300 (net) of the
estimated additional requirements under section 2, General Assembly and Economic
and Social Council affairs and conference management ($4,944,200), section 23,
Human rights ($392,700), section 27, Public information ($221,900), and section
28E, Administration, Geneva ($339,500), cannot be absorbed within the approved
appropriation of the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009. Therefore,
additional appropriation would be sought with regard to the amount of $5,898,300
(net).A/C.3/63/L.77

resolutions and decisions adopted by the Human Rights Council at its ninth
session would require: (a) an amount of $1,681,700 for which provisions have
already been foreseen under sections 2, 23 and 28E of the programme budget for
the biennium 2008-2009; and (b) an amount of $467,600 for which provisions
have not been foreseen under the programme budget for the biennium 2008-2009,
but which will be absorbed to the extent possible and will be reported in the
context of the second performance report for the biennium 2008-2009.
