               INDONESIA – RECOURSE TO ARTICLE 22.2 OF THE DSU IN THE
                           US – CLOVE CIGARETTES DISPUTE

                                REQUEST TO JOIN CONSULTATIONS


                                     Communication from Brazil


The following communication, dated 30 June 2014, from the delegation of Brazil to the delegation
of Indonesia, the delegation of the European Union and to the Chairperson of the Dispute
Settlement Body, is circulated in accordance with Article 4.11 of the DSU.

                                        _______________


Pursuant to Article 4.11 of the DSU, I would like to inform you that Brazil considers it has a
substantial trade interest in the following dispute: Indonesia – Recourse to article 22.2 of the DSU
in the US – Clove cigarettes dispute (Complainant: European Union), DS481.

As you recall, Brazil was a Third Party in the US – Clove cigarettes dispute and has also requested
to be a Third Party in the arbitration under Article 22.6. Additionally, Brazil has a systemic interest
in the legal issues raised in this dispute, which will raise matters of procedural and textual
interpretation of clear importance to WTO Members.

In light of the above, I have been instructed to express Brazil's desire to be joined in the
consultations requested by the European Union in document WT/DS481/1. I look forward to
receiving Indonesia's positive reply to this request.
