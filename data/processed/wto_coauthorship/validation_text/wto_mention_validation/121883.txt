            BRAZIL – CERTAIN MEASURES CONCERNING TAXATION AND CHARGES

                                REQUEST TO JOIN CONSULTATIONS


                                   Communication from Argentina


The following communication, dated 16 January 2014, from the delegation of Argentina to the
delegation of Brazil, the delegation of the European Union and the Chairperson of the Dispute
Settlement Body, is being circulated in accordance with Article 4.11 of the DSU.

                                        _______________


On instructions from my government and pursuant to Article 4.11 of the Understanding on Rules
and Procedures Governing the Settlement of Disputes (DSU), I hereby notify Argentina's desire
to be joined in the consultations requested by the European Union in Brazil – Certain Measures
Concerning Taxation and Charges (document WT/DS472/1).

Argentina is one of the leading exporters to the Brazilian market, inter alia in the sectors identified
in the request for consultations. In fact, Argentina's exports to Brazil totalled US$14.727 billion
during the period January–November 2013.

Consequently, Argentina has a substantial trade interest in these consultations, and therefore
looks forward to receiving a positive reply to its request.
