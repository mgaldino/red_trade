                 Response to Measures Proposed in Joint Statement by Brazil, China,
                         Ecuador, Egypt, India, Pakistan and South Africa


I.      INTRODUCTION

1.      At the meeting of the Committee on Budget, Finance and Administration (CBFA) on
11 March 2010, the Secretariat presented its first Annual Report on Diversity in the Secretariat
(WT/BFA/W/195). A Joint Statement on the Report on Diversity by Brazil, China, Ecuador, Egypt,
India, Pakistan and South Africa, was also tabled. The purpose of this document is to provide a
response to the measures proposed in the joint statement.

2.       The discussion in the Committee on Budget, Finance and Administration following the
presentation of the first annual report on diversity was of great value to the WTO Secretariat. Several
practical and useful suggestions were made, for example, in relation to increasing outreach to attract
the best qualified candidates from Members as a way to further continue to broaden the diversification
of the Secretariat staff.

3.      The Secretariat and the Director-General place great importance on diversity and will work to
ensure that the positive trends highlighted in the Secretariat's Annual Report on Diversity are not only
continued but reinforced over time. Increasing the pool of highly qualified applicants from the full
Membership is key to achieving this and developing a stronger partnership with Members will greatly
enhance the potential for success in this area.
