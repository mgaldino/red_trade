                CHINA – MEASURES RELATING TO THE PRODUCTION
              AND EXPORTATION OF APPAREL AND TEXTILE PRODUCTS

                                    Request to Join Consultations

                                     Communication from Brazil


         The following communication, dated 29 October 2012, from the delegation of Brazil to the
delegation of China, the delegation of Mexico and to the Chairperson of the Dispute Settlement Body,
is circulated in accordance with Article 4.11 of the DSU.

                                         _______________


        Pursuant to Article 4.11 of the DSU, I would like to inform you that Brazil considers it has a
substantial trade interest in the following dispute: China – Measures Relating to the Production and
Exportation of Apparel and Textile Products - DS 451.

         In light of the above, I have been instructed to express Brazil's desire to be joined in the
consultations requested by the Mexico in document WT/DS451/1. I look forward to receiving China's
reply to this request.
