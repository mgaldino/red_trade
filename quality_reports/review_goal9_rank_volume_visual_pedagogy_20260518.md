## Review Sequence

Initial visual review graded the first corrected draft B+. The blockers were oversized panel titles, an unclear Panel C axis label, and insufficient signaling that lower rank values mean higher export-destination importance. The figure was revised by reducing title hierarchy, changing Panel B to `Rank (1 = top)`, and moving Panel C to a clear `US$ billions` scale.

Initial pedagogy review graded the draft A- with no blockers, conditional on a caption defining the dashed 2009 line, the relevant competitor, Panel C color coding, units, and rank direction.

## Final Visual Audit

Grade: A.

Verdict: ready to enter the `targets` pipeline.

No blockers. The reviewer found that title hierarchy, Panel C unit clarity, rank-axis interpretation, cropping, paper-scale readability, and color encoding all pass. The only nonblocking note was that the manuscript caption should state that orange bars indicate positive margins and gray bars indicate negative margins.

## Final Pedagogy Review

Grade: A.

Verdict: ready to enter the `targets` pipeline.

No blockers. The reviewer found that the labels, color logic, and three-panel structure support the intended rank-versus-volume timing-test message without visually implying causal identification.

## Implementation Requirements Carried Forward

The manuscript caption for the main rank-versus-volume figure must define:

- the dashed 2009 line as the year China became Brazil's largest export destination;
- the relevant competitor as the destination China had to surpass, or the second-ranked destination after China became first;
- orange bars as positive margins and gray bars as negative margins;
- Panel C units as US$ billions based on the ITPD-E trade values;
- Panel B rank direction, where 1 is the top export-destination rank;
- the figure's status as descriptive timing evidence, not a causal estimate.
